Reviewer privacy policy

Last updated 11 October 2026.

Reviewer is a personal test app made by Joe Campo. It runs only on Joe's own computer and is not offered to the public.

What Reviewer can see in Google

Reviewer asks Google for one permission, "drive.file". With it, Reviewer can see and change only the Google Drive files it created or that the person signing in chose to open with it. It cannot see the rest of anyone's Drive, email or contacts.

What Reviewer keeps

Reviewer keeps the text, comments and history of those files, and the Google sign-in it needs, on Joe's computer. To suggest edits to a script, Reviewer may send that script's text to Claude, an AI assistant made by Anthropic. Apart from that, it does not send them to anyone else, sell them, or use them for advertising.

Removing access

Anyone who signed in to Reviewer can remove its access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions go to the support email shown on Google's sign-in screen for Reviewer.
